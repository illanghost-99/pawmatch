# PawMatch Support

PawMatch is a Swedish app for dog owners. You can find nearby dogs for friendship or for breeding, chat after a match, and create a written agreement.

## Contact
Write to us in the app: Profil, then Kundtjänst.
Developer: Iliamo Hanna, Upplands Väsby, Sweden.

## Your account
You can delete your account in the app under Profil, then Radera konto.
To get a copy of your data, contact us from the email on your account.

## Premium
You can cancel PawMatch Premium anytime in iPhone Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions.
The price and any free period are shown in the app before you confirm.

## Age
PawMatch is for people who are 18 or older.
